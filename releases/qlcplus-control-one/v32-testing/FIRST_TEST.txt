V32 FIRST TEST - EXACT PINNED WINDOWS HOST AND YOUR RIG

Do these in order and record pass/fail plus the fixture or control that failed.
Known source issues are listed first in README.txt. STOP is not a guaranteed
blackout while Flash overrides are active.

1. With DMX disconnected: confirm QLC+ 5.3.0 GIT a124abe, no missing fixtures,
   SoundSwitch Hardware and OS2L available, and all four universe routes agree
   with README.txt. Switch to the Live Virtual Console page.
2. Connect one low-risk LED fixture on your intended output. Start at low
   Global intensity. Prove QLC+'s native Grand Master blackout and Global zero
   actually darken it, including during a WHITE/color override, then restore.
   Do not proceed with the full rig if your blackout method fails.
3. In VirtualDJ play a known steady track using direct OS2L. Confirm QLC+'s
   reported tempo agrees and loops advance without burst-rate BPM jumps.
4. Check manual pads by mouse and Control One in every bank: latch, release
   the physical pad, same-pad off, and replacement by another pad.
5. Auto Bank sequential: while loop 6 plays, select 3. The next selections
   should be 4, 5, 6. Repeat Auto All and random; selected loop starts exactly,
   then random continues. Change speed 0.25x through 4x independently of dwell.
6. After confirming fixture modes/addresses, add one fixture class at a time.
   Check Global and Groups 1 IR-4 / 2 Wash / 3 tubes / 4 Focus independently.
   Check all four banks plus representative still and moving Priority Looks.
7. Apply/release a Priority Look over Autoplay: it owns the full visible frame,
   and release reveals the advancing underlying loop. Check rapid handoffs.
8. Test all nine colors as latches and Shift-held overrides, release Shift
   before releasing the key, and repeat over Priority. Look for stuck holds
   and the documented Wash/tube brightness-pattern flattening. Check WHITE,
   BLACK and UV hold plus latch overlap and the documented cancellation issue.
9. With Focus clearance and aims confirmed, test MOVE, position latch over
   MOVE, release, then STROBE over a loop/Priority/color hold. Watch between-look
   wheel/gobo transitions for unwanted intermediate selections. Check STOP,
   then independently apply your already-verified blackout if light remains.
10. Disconnect/reconnect Control One: transient holds should release, mouse
    controls remain available, MIDI and known LEDs return. Recheck physical
    output after the operator reconnects it. Close/reopen your saved test copy
    and confirm its routes and profiles persist.

Before relying on this for a gig, the exact intended VirtualDJ/audio, OS2L,
controller, USB/DMX output and complete rig need a combined sustained rehearsal,
including blackout and recovery. The project records specify two hours. A
short successful first test is not gig qualification. Keep the working copy
and untouched rollback together with your observation record.
