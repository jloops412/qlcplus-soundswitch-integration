# Community Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We welcome DJs, lighting operators, developers, testers, and documentation
contributors. Participate with patience, technical honesty, and respect.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing evidence and tradeoffs without attacking people;
- respecting different experience levels and accessibility needs;
- protecting private event, client, device, and account information;
- stating test boundaries honestly, especially around physical output; and
- prioritizing operator and audience safety.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, unsafe testing pressure,
or knowingly false qualification claims.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or
other participation that violates this standard, and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately through the method in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not post sensitive personal details publicly.

Maintainers will consider the context, impact, and safety of the community when
responding.
