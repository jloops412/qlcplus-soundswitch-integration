The V22 alpha-candidate workspace, profile, plug-in, installer, rollback, package test, and hashes are under `releases/qlcplus-control-one/v22/`. V21 remains the reliability rollback and V20 the protected creative rollback. Plug-in source is under `qlcplus/plugins/soundswitch/`; the deterministic V22 workspace merge is under `qlcplus/workspace-tools/`.

Historical handoffs under `docs/handoffs/` explain how the native QLC+ direction was chosen. They are retained for provenance, but this page and the V22 documents are authoritative when they differ.
